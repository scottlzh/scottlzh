This research studies how transportation systems absorb, respond to, and recover from disruptions such as tropical cyclones, severe storms, and infrastructure failures.

Representative work includes port disruption analysis using multiple-source datasets, recovery-time prediction, hazard screening and resilience measure selection, freight system resilience, and decision-support tools for infrastructure owners and operators.
