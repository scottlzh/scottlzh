This research direction develops AI systems that combine structured transportation data, text, images, video, domain knowledge, and simulation to support intelligent transportation operations.

Current topics include multimodal large language model benchmarks for disaster and port operations, agentic reasoning over reconnaissance reports, knowledge-grounded retrieval and verification, and AI-enabled safety analysis for complex mobility environments.
