**Sponsor:** Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), Exploratory Advanced Research (EAR) Program  
**Project:** Computer Vision and Edge Computing for Extraction of Anonymized Pedestrian Activity Data from Video  
**Role:** Co-PI  
**Period:** 2026-2028  
**Award:** $250,000 total; $80,000 Li portion

This project develops privacy-preserving computer vision and edge-computing methods to extract pedestrian activity information from video for transportation safety and operations. Collaborating institutions include FHWA-supported project partners and Texas A&M University.
