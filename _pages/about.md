The **Zihao Li Lab** is a transportation systems research group led by Dr. Zihao (Scott) Li in the Department of Civil, Construction & Environmental Engineering at Marquette University.

Our research develops data-driven, AI-enabled, and cyber-physical methods for transportation systems that must remain safe, resilient, and reliable under uncertainty. We study connected and automated vehicles, multimodal and agentic AI for intelligent transportation systems, traffic flow theory, active traffic safety, and infrastructure resilience under natural hazards and operational disruptions.

## Research Themes

- **Multimodal and agentic AI for intelligent transportation systems:** knowledge-grounded models, multimodal benchmarks, and decision-support systems for mobility operations.
- **Cyber-physical-human transportation resilience:** resilience analysis and recovery modeling for ports, freight systems, roads, and critical infrastructure exposed to extreme events.
- **Connected and automated transportation systems:** vehicle control, platoon stability, mixed autonomy traffic, and sensing or communication cyberattack impacts.
- **Traffic flow theory and modeling:** analytical and data-driven models for mixed traffic, adaptive cruise control, and signalized intersections.
- **Active traffic safety analysis and intervention:** crash risk estimation, digital-twin safety analysis, and intervention strategies for safer transportation networks.

Prospective students and collaborators with overlapping interests are encouraged to review the research projects and openings pages.
